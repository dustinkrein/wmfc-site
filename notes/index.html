The questionWhen K–12 funding follows the student, how do colleges and universities respond? And who is leading?
Workforce

On January 1, a federal tax credit starts paying for tutoring. No university prepares tutors for it.

The new federal scholarship tax credit can cover tutoring and after-school programs for public school students, not only private tuition. Thirty-one states have opted in. The workforce it pays for has no degree path.

Field Note 17 · October 2026
Workforce

Who prepares the teachers of microschools, classical schools and Christian schools? Mostly organizations that publish no counts.

We found twelve organizations preparing educators for the schools portable funding pays for. Three publish a number of people prepared. One reports to a state.

Field Note 16 · October 2026
Institutions

On the ESA vendor lists, universities are a rounding error.

Texas lists 5,648 approved vendors for its new ESA program. About ten belong to colleges, and most of those are campus child-care centers.

Field Note 15 · October 2026
The seam

Nine campus centers study the new schools. None prepares their teachers.

Research on hybrid schools, homeschooling and microschools now has a home on campus. Preparing the people who teach in them does not.

Field Note 14 · October 2026
The seam

Arizona funds families to choose private schools. Its teacher scholarship does not count private-school teaching.

More than 100,000 students use Arizona's ESA. The state's own tuition-free teacher program requires graduates to teach in a public or charter school.

Field Note 13 · October 2026
Institutions

A university-run school that turns down the voucher money.

Nova Southeastern's own PreK–12 school says state scholarships would limit its independence. It is the first documented refusal in the Index.

Field Note 12 · October 2026
Institutions

We said 70 universities showed no response. We had searched the wrong building in 17 of them.

A second search, outside the education schools, found universities running, founding or authorizing K–12 schools. The share with no response fell from about two in three to about half.

Field Note 11 · October 2026
Workforce

Florida's largest teacher preparer is not a university.

Florida's colleges and universities finished 60% fewer new teachers in 2023–24 than in 2010–11. District and charter programs held steady, and now finish more than any single university.

Field Note 10 · October 2026
The seam

A Florida district needed a school model. It bought one from a microschool operator.

Polk County contracted a private microschool founded by two teachers who had left a Polk County public school. The Index has coded 21 Florida institutions and found no education-school program built for this sector. Both facts describe the same state in the same year.

Field Note 09 · August 2026
Institutions

Three-quarters of Florida districts now sell to students they do not enroll

District participation in Florida's part-time scholarship marketplace went from roughly one in three to about 75% of 67 districts in twelve months. Texas districts, offered state money for a comparable arrangement, mostly declined. The contrast is the finding.

Field Note 08 · August 2026
The seam

In much of the Americas, the money follows the teacher

Coding North and South America turned up a mechanism the school-choice literature barely names: public money paid not per pupil, but against a private school's teaching payroll. Five systems in this hemisphere work that way.

Field Note 07 · August 2026
Institutions

Florida built a second door, and we walked past it

A 2021 law let Florida colleges sponsor charter schools. Four institutions now do. We had coded one of them "nothing found" — because we read its course roster and stopped.

Field Note 06 · August 2026
Institutions

The program that was built and stopped

Every other null in the Index records an institution that never built for this sector. Nova Southeastern built one and no longer offers it — documented in the university's own archive.

Field Note 05 · August 2026
Institutions

Eight authorizers, one program

Michigan is the deepest university-charter ecosystem in the country. All eight public university authorizers are now coded. Between them they hold well over 200 schools and exactly one preparation program.

Field Note 04 · August 2026
The seam

Nobody owns the workforce question

Demand is measured. Policy adoption is measured. Preparation quality is measured. Whether the workforce exists to staff what policy enables falls between every organization in the field.

Field Note 03 · July 2026
Institutions

Where universities engage, they operate

Most confirmed university entries into the choice sector took the form of running or authorizing a school rather than building a degree. A catalog scan would miss all of them.

Field Note 02 · July 2026
Workforce

The 53-point preparedness cliff

87% of teachers feel prepared to teach in a district school. 34% feel prepared for a microschool — the sector now serving more than 750,000 children.
