Field Notes

Short findings, between editions.

A single number, institution or pattern, with its evidence and its limits. Every note has a permanent address so it can be cited, checked and argued with.

The seam

A Florida district needed a school model. It bought one from a microschool operator.

Polk County contracted a private microschool founded by two teachers who had left a Polk County public school. The Index has coded 21 Florida institutions and found no education-school program built for this sector. Both facts describe the same state in the same year.

Institutions

Three-quarters of Florida districts now sell to students they do not enroll

District participation in Florida's part-time scholarship marketplace went from roughly one in three to about 75% of 67 districts in twelve months. Texas districts, offered state money for a comparable arrangement, mostly declined. The contrast is the finding.

The seam

In much of the Americas, the money follows the teacher

Coding North and South America turned up a mechanism the school-choice literature barely names: public money paid not per pupil, but against a private school's teaching payroll. Five systems in this hemisphere work that way.

Florida built a second door, and we walked past it

A 2021 law let Florida colleges sponsor charter schools. Four institutions now do. We had coded one of them "nothing found" — because we read its course roster and stopped.

The program that was built and stopped

Every other null in the Index records an institution that never built for this sector. Nova Southeastern built one and no longer offers it — documented in the university's own archive.

Field Note 05 · August 2026

Eight authorizers, one program

Michigan is the deepest university-charter ecosystem in the country. All eight public university authorizers are now coded. Between them they hold well over 200 schools and exactly one preparation program.

Nobody owns the workforce question

Demand is measured. Policy adoption is measured. Preparation quality is measured. Whether the workforce exists to staff what policy enables falls between every organization in the field.

Where universities engage, they operate

Most confirmed university entries into the choice sector took the form of running or authorizing a school rather than building a degree. A catalog scan would miss all of them.

The 53-point preparedness cliff

87% of teachers feel prepared to teach in a district school. 34% feel prepared for a microschool — the sector now serving more than 750,000 children.
