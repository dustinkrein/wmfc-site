06

Where the line is drawn

For policymakers and program leaders: the clauses that decide where a Florida teacher can train and who can mentor them. What each says, not what it should say.

s. 1004.04(5)(d), F.S.

Who picks the training sites

Programs select field sites "in cooperation with district school boards and approved private school associations." Sites must show "commitment to the education of public school students."

s. 1004.04(5)(b), F.S.

Who may supervise a student teacher

A Florida professional certificate, three years of PreK–12 teaching, an effective or highly effective rating on a performance evaluation, and clinical educator training. Those ratings come from district and charter evaluation systems; UCF also accepts “or the equivalent.”

s. 1012.555, F.S.

Who may sponsor a teacher apprentice

School districts, charter schools and charter management organizations. Private schools that take state scholarships are not on the list.

Rule 6A-5.0661, 2027

How a student teacher is judged

With "tools aligned to the district instructional personnel evaluation system approved by the Department of Education."
