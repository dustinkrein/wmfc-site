Two tier systems, two meanings

The Readiness Index codes institutions by what they have built. The Atlas codes countries by how far our research has got. They share the word "tier" and nothing else. We are renaming the Atlas scale to Coded / Shortlisted / Not yet coded to remove the ambiguity.

How to cite

Cite the edition and the date you accessed it — the datasets are living documents and we date every row for exactly this reason.

When Money Follows the Child. The WMFC Readiness Index, 2026 Baseline. whenmoneyfollowsthechild.org. Accessed [date].
When Money Follows the Child. The Portable-Funding Atlas, 2026 Baseline. whenmoneyfollowsthechild.org. Accessed [date].

Terms

  • Free to use, including commercially. Attribution appreciated, not demanded.
  • Please carry the caveats. If you cite a Tier 0 row, cite it as "nothing found," not "nothing exists" — the distinction is the point.
  • Tell us when we're wrong. Corrections are published on the Method page, never absorbed silently. info@whenmoneyfollowsthechild.org
A note on the raw files

The JSON links above open the machine-readable files directly — useful if you're pulling them into code, dense if you're reading. The CSV buttons give you a spreadsheet instead, and the site pages give you the same data laid out for humans.
