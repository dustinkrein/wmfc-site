Florida and Michigan, side by side

The two states coded in full. One dot per institution, colored by what it has built. Hover or tap a dot.

Where institutions engage, they authorize schools. They do not build programs.

Florida's Tier 1 rows are all charter sponsors. Michigan's eight university authorizers hold well over 200 schools between them, some since 1994. Across all of them: one preparation program.

Holding schools did not produce programs.

Proximity, subsidy and access were each present in Michigan without a single new course.
