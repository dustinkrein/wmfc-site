How responses are coded

Every response gets a direction. The direction records what an institution did, not whether it was right.

CompeteBuilds or runs an alternative that takes portable funds
SupplySells services or preparation to whoever is buying
FortifyStrengthens the existing public system
Open doorRemoves a barrier for educators headed outside districts
Studies itResearches or debates the policy without acting in the market
None observedLooked for, not found. Never "nothing there"
The Readiness Index

Four categories, by what was built

  • Built for the sector: stated purpose is to prepare, credential, support or operate education outside district schools.
  • Names the sector: institutional text names private, charter or non-traditional settings.
  • Incidental: reaches these educators only as a side effect.
  • Nothing found: absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

Operating and authorizing schools is coded, not just programs. Before any institution is coded "nothing found," the state's charter sponsor registry is checked.

The university scan

Innovation score, and why it is separate

Each university's two strongest education initiatives are scored on scale, evidence and novelty (0–5 each). Score = 2 × novelty + scale + evidence, max 20. The portfolio is the mean of the top two; a missing second counts as zero. Response to portable funding is coded separately, so innovation never stands in for response.

04

Limits that bind hard

  • Cases were found by searching for engagement. That samples on the outcome and biases every explanation offered.
  • Coverage is uneven. Florida and Michigan are complete. Six states carry a single placeholder row. Read every state total against its depth.
  • Published text only. Coding rests on program pages, catalogs and news, not interviews. An institution that considered a program and declined looks the same as one that never considered it.
  • The university scan is a first pass. 102 universities, public sources from 2025–2026. "None observed" means not found in that scan. The 31 Most Innovative universities outside the AAU are coded for response but not yet scored.
  • Florida's state colleges are not yet scanned. 25 of the 28 Florida College System institutions remain unread.
  • Single points in time. This field moves fast. Every row carries the date it was checked.

Corrections

What was wrong, what is right, and what it changes. Dated and permanent. No silent edits.

Correction 03 · October 2026

We said 0 of 102 universities prepare microschool educators. Arizona State does.

Our first-pass scan coded ASU on ASU Prep Digital's approval as an Arizona ESA vendor and searched for microschool preparation in education schools only. ASU Prep Global, ASU's K–12 unit, runs a Microschool Entrepreneur Fellowship that trains parents, educators and school leaders to open microschools: pedagogy, curriculum, financial modeling, team recruitment, an in-person retreat in Tempe and months of coaching. The homepage now reads 1 of 102. The size of the finding barely moves. Its location does: the one program we found sits outside the education school, the same pattern as every other university response in the scan. Hybrid-school and homeschool co-op preparation remain none found. One near-miss we checked and did not count: the Micro Schools Network certificate pairs Harvard's general Instructional Leadership Certificate with a microschool course written by MSN. Harvard's part is not built for microschools, so Harvard's coding is unchanged. Before we write "none found" for a university, we now search its K–12, online and outreach units, not only its education school.

Correction 02 · August 2026

We coded the University of West Florida "nothing found." It is a registered charter school sponsor.

Our July pass read only UWF's School of Education program roster. UWF is listed in the state's Directory of Charter School Sponsoring Entities, and its trustees approved it as authorizer of a Somerset Academy elementary school. The row is now coded built for the sector. The state sponsor registry is now checked before any institution is coded "nothing found." That change also surfaced Miami Dade College and Pasco-Hernando State College.

Correction 01 · July 2026

We claimed no one had scanned colleges of education for choice-sector programs. EdChoice had.

Surfing the Pipeline by Michael Q. McShane (October 2023) scanned Florida, Arizona, Wisconsin and Vermont, and is the source of the 34% preparedness figure. Our baseline now builds on it with attribution.
