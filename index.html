Latest

Workforce

Florida is rebuilding the core of teacher preparation. The new rule never names the schools half its students now choose.

From August 2027 every new Florida teacher must take three state-defined courses. The rule that defines them uses the words private, charter, home education, choice and scholarship zero times. Of Florida's 40 public universities and state colleges, 7 name private schools as a place to train.

Field Note 18 · November 2026
Workforce

On January 1, a federal tax credit starts paying for tutoring. No university prepares tutors for it.

The new federal scholarship tax credit can cover tutoring and after-school programs for public school students, not only private tuition. Thirty states are on the IRS list of participants. The workforce it pays for has no degree path.

Field Note 17 · October 2026
Workforce

Who prepares the teachers of microschools, classical schools and Christian schools? Mostly organizations that publish no counts.

We found twelve organizations preparing educators for the schools portable funding pays for. Three publish a number of people prepared. One reports to a state.

Field Note 16 · October 2026
Institutions

On the ESA vendor lists, universities are a rounding error.

Texas lists 5,648 approved vendors for its new ESA program. About ten belong to colleges, and most of those are campus child-care centers.

Correction 08 · November 2026

We missed a twelfth route into teaching, called UWF’s placement policy silent when its catalog names private schools, and coded UNF open from a list that is not a placement policy.

Florida has twelve certification routes, not eleven: a classical certificate created in 2024 is valid only at a classical school. We also recoded two placement policies: UWF’s catalog names private schools, and the UNF list we cited belongs to a research institute, not student teaching.
